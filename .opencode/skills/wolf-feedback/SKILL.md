# Feedback and work improvement

Establish the observation, sufficient grounds, responsible party. Use delivered
context; one question can replace an investigation. The skill does not change
role rights, the goal or acceptance criteria; analyzing the whole session is
not obligatory.

| Observation | Action | Result |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Missing input or decision | Ask the lead with context and consequence | A concrete question, no failure quota |
| Tool failure/inconvenience | FRICTION and return to the requester; use delivered means | A workaround, a fix or a constraint |
| A one-off deviation | Fix within scope or return a remark | Correction of the current result |
| A proven bad general principle W1–W4 | [Filing and triage](references/complaints.md) | One complaint with a factual source |
| Unknown cause affecting the result | The lead assigns an [episode diagnosis](references/session-diagnosis.md) | Findings/evidence/gaps and the further route |

The executor records the relevant fact, returns a RESULT, without widening the
mandate or waiting for a rule fix: automatic processing by the Steward is
temporarily disabled; the signal is recorded and is not lost. When recording
is forbidden, pass rule/evidence/proposal to the lead without invented ID;
the lead answers with context/means/a decision, triages, passes a complaint
about their own or a higher level up. The lead organizes the diagnosis and
does not investigate logs personally. Evidence is preserved through an
authorized channel; the skill is changed by the owner in their contour,
requesting the missing through the dispatcher.

One reproducible W1–W4 is enough; do not require three failures or lessons
maturity. The complaint target is set by the rule's source, not automatically
by the filer's role. A complaint/triage does not replace task acceptance. A
known fact needs no deep diagnosis to file. Do not turn every FRICTION or
working note into a complaint.

In further feedback, state the original complaint and actual rule version, if
known. Verification of the changed skill and effect measurement connect once
contracts are implemented; a new mode/status does not arise from a single
instruction.
