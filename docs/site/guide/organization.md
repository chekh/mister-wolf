# Organization keeps the goal in view

The two-level organization exists to maintain direction over a long assignment: mr-wolf holds the user's intent, clarifies the goal and accepts results — and it performs part of the work itself. For specialized or parallel work it hires one of the eight wolf-\* specialists directly; executors do not delegate further. A local difficulty returns to the participant holding the needed context; acceptance connects work back to the original goal. Memory carries recorded state across sessions. The Steward improves methods through a separate, cross-cutting loop (temporarily paused in 3.0.0).

## Responsibility

| Level       | Owns                                                               | Boundary                                                                                                                   |
| ----------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------ | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Wolf — lead | Intent, goal, success criteria, communication and final acceptance | Performs subject work on the goal itself; hires wolf-\* executors directly; retains decisions and permitted Git operations |

| wolf-\* executor | One finite result or research question | Does not expand scope, spawn other agents or delegate further |

Frames combine instructions with harness permissions. Some boundaries are permission-enforced; others are behavioral protocols. A prohibition in text is not proof that every action is machine-validated.

## Working cycle

| Step                   | Responsible             | Result                                                        |
| ---------------------- | ----------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Goal and brief         | User and Wolf           | Intent, scope and acceptance criteria                         |
| Plan                   | Mr. Wolf                | Finite tasks, dependencies, assigned methods and verification |
| Execution              | wolf-\* executors       | Verifiable partial results or specific questions              |
| Integration and report | Mr. Wolf                | Combined result, criteria checks and limitations              |
| Acceptance             | Wolf                    | Verdict against the goal and evidence                         |
| Checkpoint             | Responsible participant | Significant state and next step recorded in memory            |

A checkpoint is also useful inside long work: before handoff, after a significant milestone, at a difficulty or risky decision. It preserves recorded meaning; unrecorded findings can be lost, and automatic process recovery is not provided.

For authored documents the route adds a readability editing pass before substantive review: the author's text goes to a dedicated wolf-editor pass (wolf-document-readability), which preserves content and accuracy. The pass is not substantive review (wolf-review) and not wolf-user-report's standalone human document — the review loop stays where it was.

## Compose an assistant and choose a session

Mr. Wolf hires an executor from the wolf-\* set with an appropriate face, assigned skills, inputs and tools. The brief supplies the result, contribution to the goal, criteria, boundaries, sources, methods, verification, budget and return condition. Implementation, research and review profiles receive only applicable fields; a missing required input comes back as a question.

The role does not determine the model or its reasoning depth. Model/variant selection uses actual host capabilities; a wish in a brief does not prove the setting was applied. The `standard|draft` density shortens service text while preserving substantial evidence and checks.

After deciding to delegate, mr-wolf chooses `fresh` for a new independently acceptable result, `continue` for the same unaccepted result, or `replace` for a replacement on that task. Before replacement, stop writing, preserve the result and transfer ownership. A timeout does not prove writing stopped. Clean context depends on actual history inheritance by the host; limitations must be explicit. Small related operations are grouped into one finite executor assignment rather than split further.

An executor instance owns a bounded delivery, not the entire goal, thread or user session. After that delivery is accepted, the next independently acceptable result gets a fresh executor even when work is sequential and the topic stays the same. Corrections to the same unaccepted result may continue with the existing executor. The new executor receives relevant artifacts, evidence, constraints and decisions rather than the previous chat history.

When distributing a goal across several deliveries needs substantive planning, mr-wolf prepares bounded briefs, dependencies, parallel launch conditions and an integration owner itself, or commissions one bounded planning assignment. Ready independent deliveries may run in parallel within the overall budget and host capabilities. A small coherent delivery needs no separate planner. Shared plans and memory have one assigned writer; existing verification and acceptance gates remain in force.

## Acceptance

The report connects criteria to artifacts, verification commands, exit codes and material limitations. Where needed, retain revisions/hashes, independent review and unresolved risks. Executor self-checks do not replace required independent acceptance. Significant risk may require a separate reviewer before Wolf's final verdict.

## Council

A council is a bounded consultation method for hard forks: uncertainty, high risk, irreversibility, conflicting evidence or stalled progress. `wolf-expert` holds a bounded position and `wolf council record` preserves the packet; see [Expert Council](/guide/council). Synthesis preserves accepted claims, rejected alternatives and dissent; the authorized owner still decides. Budget and rounds are bounded.

## Improve the organization

The working cycle retains experience. The governed loop handles supported feedback and versions faces under the authorized protocol; in 3.0.0 the Steward behind it is temporarily out of standard routing, signals are recorded, and the role returns in the next release. It is not an execution level. Methods can change while history and owner control remain.
