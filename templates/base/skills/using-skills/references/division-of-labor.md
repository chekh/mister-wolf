# Division of labor and mandate boundaries

Loaded by the lead (L0) before assigning, continuing or replacing a subordinate. Do not
re-search current delivered text. The skill chooses the work boundaries and the executor instance;
current planning, verifications, independent review and acceptance stay in force. The frame
sets the rights: the lead (L0) carries out the work or hires an executor (L1); an executor
hires no one and creates no agents.

## Bounded mandate

Hire for a result complete in meaning and acceptable on its own, with explicit criteria,
inputs, scope, rights and budget. The lead organizes a bounded delivery itself or through an
executor performing the finite part. The shared goal, thread, topic and user session do not
set an instance's lifetime. Related actions, verifications and fixes may form one delivery;
create no agent per file, command or stage. Do not merge independent deliveries into a
"whole project" or "everything until the session ends" mandate for convenience.

Before the next hire, distinguish:

| Choice | Basis |
| --- | --- |
| fresh | A new result acceptable on its own → a new instance and a selected TASK; also for sequential work by one requester. |
| continue | The same unaccepted result → the previous instance: a question answered, an unblock, a planned delivery part or a findings fix. |
| replace | Same mandate, but the executor is unavailable or the context is unsuitable → a new instance via wolf-handoff. |

A new TASK name does not refresh an old session; a task_id matching a tool parameter is no
session identifier. Continue does not extend scope. After accepting an independent result, add
no further delivery to the previous executor, even if project and topic match. An intermediate
approval inside a pre-bounded mandate does not close the whole mandate automatically. Report an
unavailable fresh/close or host history inheritance as a constraint; do not pass a new label off
as a context reset or bypass permissions.

A new independent part, an accepted previous mandate, new scope/AC/decision, new research or no
progress → a fresh executor instance. Reuse only for a local correction of the last unaccepted
result if the previous goal/AC/decisions are unchanged, the work is concrete and bounded, no
research is needed, progress was made and retained context gives a named advantage. After one
corrective return, the next pass always goes to a fresh instance. Record the REUSE basis and
counter in the TASK. "Author", "knows the project", "more convenient" are not bases.

## Preparation and hiring

Assessment and planning are existing work kinds, not four mandatory calls per hire. Use
sufficient inputs and the accepted plan. If distributing the shared goal among several
executors needs subject work, the lead mandates a plan under wolf-plan. The planning executor
organizes needed help and returns ready bounded briefs, dependencies/start conditions,
parallelism proposals, the total budget and the integration owner. The lead accepts the plan
against goal and rights and hires, adding no technical decomposition; a gap returns to the plan
author. A plan is a separate result when planning itself was mandated; accepting it does not
permit implementation. A small, clear delivery needs no separate planner: the lead plans its own
mandate and hires within it, and planning extends no hiring right.

Run ready independent mandates in parallel within the total budget and host capacity. Write
and shared-resource boundaries, dependencies and integration order must be known; a blocker stops
the dependent ones, not the rest. With several deliveries each belongs to its own executor; the
assigned integrator organizes technical integration and the owner keeps the final verdict. The
shared plan, memory and other mutable artifacts have one assigned writer; a change of owner goes
through handoff. The owner gets short statuses and version links, not the whole planning context;
the assigned planner keeps the detailed plan.

## Communication and completion

Apply the current agent-language.md in full and the contracts.md forms. Express a hire for an
assessment, plan or delivery through the goal/result/scope of the existing TASK; no new message
types, profiles, mandatory slots or statuses appear. References to the mandate and inputs are
addressed, by ID with a version; descriptive discussion stays free. Keep the role's RESULT forms:
REPORT/STATUS/CRITERIA for the organizer, by profile for the executor. A ready report is not
acceptance; accepting a plan is not execution. What the form does not cover is "out of contract"
under R6; a proven language difficulty is a LANGUAGE item via wolf-feedback, not a unilateral
language change.

A returned question/report ends the call, but not necessarily the mandate. The requester applies
existing gates; do not use a new session for an automatic repetition of sufficient verifications. A new executor gets
the relevant artifacts, evidence, ACTIVE_CONSTRAINTS/DECISIONS[] and open questions, not the chat
archive. Replace keeps the task_id, criteria, findings and the budget already spent by the
mandate; fresh does not cancel the total delivery limit. Mark unknown costs; never zero, and
create no new accounting mechanism.

After acceptance, save the needed results/evidence and complete the instance's use for the
mandate. Before closing/replacing, stop recording and check processes under wolf-handoff;
closing deletes no worktree or evidence. If the host cannot close a session, stop hiring it for
work and state the constraint.
