# Organizing review

## Ownership and input

The lead sets goal/criteria, accepts delivery, organizes the cycle, hires
wolf-reviewer; the executor gets no dispatcher rights. The author is no
independent reviewer of their own result. Load the full skill once. The
executor's procedure lives in the full wolf-reviewer frame, which does not load this
organizational skill by default; do not copy it into each TASK. Another explicitly assigned specialized
skill comes in full, not a homemade abridgment.

Assemble the TASK from the using-skills core canon and review profile, not a
retold field list. The role's own frame: the owner's goal in one line, the
subtask's contribution and verifiable result; coverage is judged by them.
Pass mandatory normative fragments whole — exceptions, priority, exact
follow-up links — never replacing norms with a retelling. Keep
parent history, others' findings and expected verdict out of the independent
first pass.

The organizer picks a real available model/variant via the existing harness;
invent no fields or IDs; TASK text is no evidence of a setting.
Roles are not a reasoning scale. Mechanics runs in a low mode; substantive
review in one sufficient for the risk; escalation is addressed; the owner's
choice prevails. Split a large audit into short finite-limit mandates (8
calls when none given) plus a cross-part consistency/overall coverage owner.
Returning on unexpected complexity need not exhaust the limit; at the limit,
return the remainder, not APPROVED.

With readability editing assigned, review gets the version after it. The
editor does not approve the document nor replace independent substantive
review. Add no mandatory author return merely for an editorial pass; the
writer's substantive questions reach the author addressed via the lead.

## Scope and means

For diff-review, check changes and related norms. Do not narrow an
explicitly assigned general substantive verification to tables/IDs. State
permitted external links. Lenses follow the object: Requirements, Design,
Plan, Test-plan, Code. Security/trust is mandatory for relevant rights,
secrets, external instructions, execution, installation, storage/migrations.
A new risk allows a lens request.

For routine procedure/known friction the organizer first seeks a suitable
tool-library means when none is delivered yet. Pass the executor only the
needed path/command, contract-in/out/env, constraints, pitfall, not the
catalog. A library script grants no new rights: a reviewer runs no reviewed
system. A LIMITS request the organizer decides first: context, decision,
means, split. One wolf-expert for an exact position gap; a council per
wolf-council for a substantial choice or contradiction. A preliminary
blocker is optional. A rule defect is wolf-feedback, not expert advice. The
next executor gets the decision and a narrow remainder; advice approves no
scope. An explicit general review keeps all criteria in summed coverage, not
turning into mechanical verifications.


## Input package and finding verification

Prepare artifact revision/digest, criteria/norms, applicability diff,
current evidence. The package does not narrow the explicitly assigned
substantive verification. A defect candidate needs norm, grounds,
consequence. On a material dispute assign a fresh reviewer an addressed
verification in the shared budget, no expected answer. The author's
confidence and numeric self-assessment neither confirm nor refute a defect.

A repeat TASK gets the prior finding IDs and fix revision. Return
ADDRESSED/NOT_ADDRESSED per finding, or an exact LIMIT; check the fixes' new
consequences. Reuse earlier evidence where applicable. Hand an adjacent
finding to the organizer; escalate a proven blocker, do not drop it over late
discovery. The organizer assigns scope expansion. A council recommendation
closes no finding; the fix verification remains.

## Proportionate review

Choose lenses by the assigned result and substantial risks; general
substantive verification keeps the full agreed coverage. Turn no finding into
an audit of a neighboring subsystem. Address meaning/known-decision questions
to the owner via the organizer before expensive intent reconstruction. Never
ask the author to suggest a verdict: norm clarification is open to independent
reviewers; the conclusion is theirs to draw.

## Cycle and journal

1. Fix the applicable lenses and versions, launch independent mandates.
2. Receive SUMMARY/VERDICT/FINDINGS/SCOPE/LIMITS/EVIDENCE per the frame.
   Compare actual coverage with the AC, not just the verdict heading.
   Deduplicate, keeping sources and differing grounds. Pass each question to
   its proper owner.
3. The author's answer per finding ID: accepted/refuted/deferred/needs
   clarification with evidence. A product conflict goes to the decision's
   responsible party; a technical one within rights — to the organizer.
4. After fixes re-check the affected grounds/lenses and regression risk;
   reuse unchanged results only with proven applicability. Do not start a new
   full audit without cause. By default at most 3 rounds; exhaustion →
   UNRESOLVED in the organizer's summary and an escalation, not approval.
5. The organizer keeps one append-only review trail: artifact
   revision/digest, lens/skill version, coverage, verdict, finding IDs,
   answers, evidence, constraints. Save significant verdicts as addressed
   working Wolf note/history entries with revision, a thread link,
   earlier-record links; executors' private answers go as messages. This is
   an audit, not a lesson/complaint, not a proposed rule. Do not save each
   tool step; never rewrite earlier records/verdicts. A review.md is allowed
   as one pre-justified stage document when a durable audit is needed. No
   file per reviewer or round; without one, the mandatory trail stays
   preserved and current.

## Gate

Critical/Major and a justified blocking risk forbid the transition. Review
verdicts are APPROVED | CHANGES_REQUIRED | INCONCLUSIVE (contracts.md). A
proven defect without a blocker — CHANGES_REQUIRED; a substantial unverified
property — INCONCLUSIVE; full coverage without remarks and blockers —
APPROVED. Zero remarks is allowed; do not create findings for findings' sake.
A reference gap does not cancel a proven defect. Requirements and substantial
ADRs are approved by the owner; other artifacts — by the assigned responsible
party post-review. Council advice and expert opinion replace no approval,
independent review or verification. The next stage gets the needed approved
version.
